# Accounting Evidence Checklist

## Source

- Original document identity and immutable location
- Parser/provider/version provenance
- Issuer, recipient, and direction evidence
- Header, dates, currency, totals, and payment context

## Lines and VAT

- Canonical line description, quantity, unit, net, VAT rate, and VAT amount
- VAT summary by rate
- Line, VAT, withholding, discount, and grand-total reconciliation
- Explicit `line-missing` or `insufficient-evidence` when required proof is absent

## Accounting context

- Real client chart plan and account descriptions
- Direction-filtered candidates
- Client activity and counterparty context
- Applicable accountant-approved rules and their scope

## Result

- Balanced debit and credit
- Amount-to-source trace
- Decision narrative and confidence
- Specific review reason and next action
- Export gate independent from processing status
- Authorized correction, learning event, and conflict behavior
