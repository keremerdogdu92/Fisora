# Fisero Decision Format

## Current truth

- Confirmed behavior and evidence
- Assumptions and unknowns

## Decision

One sentence naming the choice and why it matters now.

## Options

For each realistic option:

- value;
- cost;
- risk;
- reversibility;
- long-term consequence.

## Recommendation

State the recommended option directly. Explain why the other options lose.

## User decision

List only unresolved choices. The user owns the final product decision.

## Delivery contract

- Scope and non-scope
- Acceptance criteria
- Verification
- Whether commit, push, or deploy would later require approval
